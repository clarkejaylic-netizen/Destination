# DestinationGuide
### Clear Routes. Confident Substitutes. Safer Preparation.

**DestinationGuide is a web app concept designed to help school transportation departments organize route information and prepare substitute bus drivers for unfamiliar routes.**

## The Problem

Substitute school bus drivers may be assigned routes they have never driven before. Important information—such as stops, estimated arrival times, driving instructions, and potential hazards—needs to be clear, organized, and up to date.

When route information is incomplete or difficult to find, preparing for an unfamiliar route becomes more challenging.

## The Purpose

DestinationGuide aims to make route information easier to organize, review, and share. It focuses on improving the handoff of route knowledge between transportation staff and substitute drivers.

Unlike a real-time bus-tracking app, DestinationGuide focuses on **route documentation and driver preparation**.

## How It Works

DestinationGuide is built around three main functions:

### 1. Capture
Authorized staff can enter route details, including:
- Route names and vehicle information
- Stops and estimated arrival times
- Driving instructions and landmarks
- Potential hazards and special notes

### 2. Transfer
Route details can be organized into a standardized, printable guide. Substitute drivers can review the information before departure, helping them prepare for an unfamiliar route.

### 3. Notify
A notification section highlights items that may require attention, such as:
- Route changes
- Missing route information
- Guides awaiting verification

These notifications are intended to help staff identify information that needs review.

## Example Scenario

A substitute driver is assigned to a fictional route serving **Pines Middle School**.

With DestinationGuide, the route information can be organized into a guide containing the planned stops, estimated times, instructions, and caution notes. Staff can review the information and identify missing details before the guide is provided to the driver.

*All demonstration routes and details are fictional.*

## Technology

DestinationGuide is developed as a web application using:

- **HTML** — page structure
- **CSS** — styling and layout
- **Bootstrap** — interface components
- **JavaScript** — interactive features

The prototype explores printable route guides and notification interactions. Browser-based storage may be used during development.

## What Makes It Different?

DestinationGuide is not designed to track buses in real time. Its focus is the information drivers need **before a route begins**.

By bringing route details, printable documentation, and review reminders into one interface, the project explores a more organized way to prepare substitute drivers.

## Future Improvements

Future versions could include:
- Secure accounts and role-based access
- Shared route data for authorized staff and drivers
- Automatic notifications when route details change
- Route verification history
- Improved accessibility and mobile support

## Safety and Privacy

DestinationGuide is a prototype, not a replacement for official transportation procedures. Real-world use would require secure data storage, appropriate access controls, accurate route verification, and approval from the relevant school transportation department.

No real student information should be included in the demonstration.

## Project Goal

The goal of DestinationGuide is simple: **make route information clearer, more organized, and easier to review so substitute drivers can prepare with greater confidence.**

*Developed as a software project exploring practical applications of web technology to a real-world communication challenge.*
